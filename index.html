Yunqi Huang 黄允祺

One Pool Street, E20 2AF

London, UK

I am a third-year PhD Candidate in Computer Science at University College London (UCL), focusing on EIT-based Tactile Sensing Skin and Soft Robot Learning.

I am fortunate to be advised by Dr. Thomas George Thuruthel. My research aims to bridge the gap between soft materials and intelligent control, enabling robots to feel and interact with the world more naturally—much like an octopus! 🐙

Previously, I completed my Master of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering at the National University of Singapore (NUS), supervised by Prof. Chui Chee Kong.

Outside of the lab, I enjoy snowboarding 🏂 and hitting the gym 🏋️.
